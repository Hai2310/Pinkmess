# Sources and usage

- **Noto Emoji** — https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji (Google, open-source). Icons in `emoji-*` packs are rendered using the installed NotoColorEmoji font and styled with small pastel decorative marks. See `NOTO-DEBIAN-COPYRIGHT.txt` for the distribution licensing details (SIL Open Font License 1.1 for fonts, Apache 2.0 for most image resources). No font binaries are bundled.
- **Chibi illustrations** — created by AI specifically for this pack, not copied from a third-party sticker website. They are kept in `chibi-*`, `cute-*`, `mini-*`, and friendship/school packs.
- The visual styles are deliberately different between Chibi and Noto collections. Do not represent them as a single artist's work.
- Microsoft's Fluent Emoji and OpenMoji were researched but their assets are **not packaged** here.
- Do not remove the required attributions if redistributing.
